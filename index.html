Lumi In development

Lumi is one model with three capabilities. We are focusing on synthetic data generation first, because shareable, privacy-safe data is the biggest blocker in health and genomics research. The other two capabilities are being developed and will follow soon.

Synthetic data generationCreate realistic, privacy-safe rows that keep the patterns of the real data.Focus now
ClassificationPredict categories like disease status, subtype or risk group.Coming soon
RegressionPredict continuous values like lab results, scores or expression levels.Coming soon
